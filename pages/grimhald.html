GRIMHALD

INFORMATION

Adalwin, the son of a wealthy Norusian aristocrat, gets kicked out of his home. He stands to lose his inheritance if he does not complete his education under the famous & infamous explorer Grimhald.

As part of the Art Department's Branding Team I created the title design for the comic book's cover page, and concept art for two letters featured in the comic book's story.

CLIENT

Soul Atlas

ROLE

Comic Book Title Design | Concept Art

GRIMHALD TITLE

Grimhald is an action/adventure character. He's a grumpy, seasoned explorer who finds himself in the haunting - a place full of deadly demons.

The title should evoke action and rugged adventure, and a hint of darkness/mystery. Utilizing the color of his clothes, as well as the wild jungles he explores, green is chosen as the primary color of the title.
