2FACED

INFORMATION

#2FACED is hex code for this lovely shade of blue -> . Isn't it fun that you can hide words in colors. With that came the idea of "Hexing".

I first wanted to make a font, but have it function as a Grant Morrison-esque hypersigil. That by using it you are essentially casting a spell, hexing your own brand. To play off that idea, instead of being free for commercial use, this font is free for anti-commercial use.

From that font I made a logo, and from that logo I made a design alter ego for myself. This was a fun experiment.
