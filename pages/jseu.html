JOINT SINGULARITY EXPLORATION UNIT

INFORMATION

The JSEU is a fictional company/task-force in a universe inspired by Tarkovsky's "Stalker" and Benson/Moorhead's "The Endless", where localized time distortions trap people in endless loops.

The idea behind creating this Brand Style Guidebook is "What if a Brand Guidebook could be used as a storytelling medium?"

I've also included stylized lore pages for those interested.
