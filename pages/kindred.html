KINDRED SPIRITS

INFORMATION

Kindred Spirits is a fictional Distillery Company established in 1925. It was founded by eight Balkan sister-spirits combating prohibition-era temperance movements when such ventures would have been considered illegal. Their thesis was simple - Spirits are meant to be enjoyed freely, not prohibited.

"Regalia", their distinctive original spirit inspired by the Serbian plum brandy "Šljivovica", paved the way for the contemporary expanded set of eight distinct flavors, each named after a sister.

The brand remains alluring, adventureous, and deeply committed to their anti-prohibition and anti-temperance roots.

Unapologetically Spirited.

LABELS

The goal of this project was to design a label and packaging fort he 8 different brandy flavors. The design had to feel prestigious and traditional, reflecting the distillery's long history.

As such, the Art Deco style was chosen, as it is rooted in the contemporary style of when the distillery was established.

The main logo of the brand features a portrait of a woman character, suggesting the female figures that established this brand.

Each label features a representation of that brandy's fruit flavor through an art deco style abstraction of that fruit.

Each label further features an art deco patteren for decoration.
