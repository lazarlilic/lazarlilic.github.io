ARAKKI COMIC BOOK TITLES

INFORMATION

These are purely fan-created comic book titles. The Krakoan era of X-Men comics is hugely inspirational for me, and a big part of that is their graphic design-focused approach to storytelling.

The Arakki as a new cast of characters are endlessly fascinating, and because they are new and have never had a standalone comic, they provide interesting creative exercises regarding the graphic title treatments.

CLIENT

Personal

ROLE

Comic Book Title Design | Brand Design

WHITE SWORD

The title treatment for White Sword is inspired by the "W" shape of his tower and his circular chest emblem.

JON IRONFIRE

The title treatment for Jon Ironfire is inspired by his double-triangle chest emblem.

LODUS LOGOS

The title treatment for Lodus Logos is inspired by his superpower of creating metal through speaking. He only speaks in haikus, and as such creates small intricate daggers.

That's the most metal, yet most beautiful power ever. I wanted to create a metal-esque logo where the illegibility doesn't come trhough spikiness/edginess but from little magical curlicues and jewelry.
