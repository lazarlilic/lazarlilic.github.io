WHO AM I

I'm a graphic designer with a passion for storytelling. A life long obsession with comic books has taught me to be bold with creative expression, and uninhibited with whimsy. Design, for me, is an outlet to look at the ugly world and find beauty and humor in it. With the world threatened by AI, we desperately need more connection, empathy, art, and humanity. That's what motivates me, and that's what I would like to accomplish. Instead of faking professionlism, why not show personality, be spirited?

Studying at the Faculty of Technical Sciences in Novi Sad has taught me to be a Jack of all Trades, with skills ranging from Graphic Design, to Print, Web and Font Design. As of this moment I am also slowly studying Video Editing, and writing my own 78-issue Comic Book.
